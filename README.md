# Odysseek

> *The city is the playground. The story is the journey. The player is the explorer.*

**odysseek** is a real-world, location-based treasure hunt game. Players pick a city, follow a story, solve riddles to work out where to go next, and physically travel to each checkpoint to unlock the next chapter. It blends storytelling, puzzle-solving, exploration and local tourism.

Built by a team of four for **Hack N Pitch 2026**.

**Status:** concept and initial product vision. Features marked *(open)* below are still being decided.

## The idea

Most mobile games keep you on the screen. odysseek sends you out into the city. Each city is a game world, each location is a checkpoint, and each adventure is a story told through connected clues.

The goal is to make players investigate their surroundings instead of following a fixed route, and to leave them knowing a city better than when they started.

## How a hunt works

1. Choose a city and an adventure.
2. Read the storyline and the first clue.
3. Interpret the riddle and work out the destination.
4. Use the map, your live location and the distance indicator to get there.
5. Solve the clue at the location to unlock the next checkpoint.
6. Repeat until the adventure is complete (roughly ten checkpoints per adventure).

After each unlock, the game can also give **transport guidance** between checkpoints (for example, metro to a station, then an auto-rickshaw from a designated stand). Finding the destination and getting there are deliberately separate parts of the game.

## Game modes

| Mode | Who it is for | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Local** | People who know the city well | Story and riddles only. You research and identify places yourself. Recommendations are woven subtly into the narrative. |
| **Amateur / Intermediate** *(open)* | People with some familiarity | A middle ground between Local and Tourist. Mechanics still being designed. |
| **Tourist** | Visitors new to the city | Story, riddles, extra context to narrow down destinations, explicit local recommendations (what to eat, why a place matters) and more navigation help. |
| **Challenge** | Competitive players | Complete a hunt as fast as possible. No sightseeing or optional discoveries. Per-challenge leaderboards. |

### Playing with others

- **Solo:** one player completes an adventure alone.
- **Duo:** two players solve clues together.
- **Co-op:** 3-5 players collaborate on the same puzzles.
- **Head-to-head:** players or teams race to finish the same adventure first.

## Features

- **Narrative adventures:** every hunt has its own story, for example a never-delivered letter, a forgotten merchant, or a mystery tied to old establishments and heritage buildings. These are illustrative concepts, not a final line-up.
- **Riddles and clues:** some require observation, independent research or connecting several pieces of information.
- **Map and location integration:** live player position, destination position, approximate distance, navigation assistance and location-based progression. Players grant location permission to play.
- **Local discovery:** optional stops between checkpoints, such as century-old sweet shops, street-food spots, old bookstores, markets and heritage buildings.
- **Challenge leaderboards:** ranked by completion time, puzzle performance, hints used and overall score.
- **Ratings:** players rate an adventure after finishing it, and ratings appear next to each adventure.
- **Post-adventure trivia:** an optional quiz on the history, architecture and places met along the route, with its own leaderboard.

## Cities and adventures

Adventures are organised by city, similar to how BookMyShow organises events. Players pick a city and browse the hunts available there.

Candidate cities: Kolkata, Mumbai, Delhi, Lucknow, Kanpur, Chennai, and other major Indian cities.

Each city can hold several independent adventures (for example, four to six in Kolkata), each with its own story, route and area. New adventures can be added to existing cities over time.

## Business model

The primary model is **paid adventures**: each hunt is sold as its own experience.

Potential additional revenue:

- Premium access to exclusive stories and routes
- Sponsored treasure hunts
- Partnerships with tourism companies and travel agencies
- Collaborations with local businesses, restaurants, museums and heritage organisations
- Ticketing and event platforms such as BookMyShow
- Institutional and tourism-oriented experiences

Pricing and partnership structures are not finalised.
